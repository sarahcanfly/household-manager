# Intake questions (about 15 minutes)

Send these to a new family, or go through them on a call. Their answers go into
`onboarding/<family>.json` (copy `family.example.json`), and `scripts/new_family.py`
turns that into their setup files.

## The household
1. What should the app call you? (shown at the top, e.g. "Jess & Sam")
2. Who lives there or turns up on the calendar? For each: first name, and adult, kid, or "other"
   (a co-parent, grandparent or sitter who drives sometimes).
3. Which adults should sign in? Their email addresses. Who gets the weekly and reminder emails?
4. What town are you in? (for local events and the trash-holiday schedule)

## Calendars
5. School calendars: a link for each school (most school websites have "Subscribe" or an .ics link).
   Which kids go to which school?
6. What from the school calendar do you want, and what is noise?
   (e.g. "no-school days and early release only; never School Committee or high school events")
7. Co-parenting: do you share a calendar with the other parent?
   - iPhone Calendar: open the calendar's info, turn on **Public Calendar**, and share the link.
   - Google Calendar (computer): **Settings and sharing → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format**.
8. A screenshot of a couple of weeks of that calendar, so custody days can be recognised
   (e.g. "Kids @Jess's" / "Kids @Alex's"). Which titles mean the kids are with you?

## Preferences
9. What kinds of outings and events do you like? Ages of the kids? Live music?
10. When should the weekly email arrive? Evening reminder time? A 7am "today" notification?
11. Any repeating reminders? (trash night, recycling week, pay the sitter)

## Phones (on setup day)
12. iPhone or Android for each adult? Do they want the "Ask Household" Siri shortcut?
